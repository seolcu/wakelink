Why it rings even on silent

Your clock app's alarm still goes off when your phone is on silent. That's because silent mode only mutes ringtones and notification sounds, not the alarm volume.

WakeLink rings on that same alarm channel, so it gets through silent and vibrate mode without any special permission.

Do Not Disturb's "Total silence" mutes alarms too. If you want WakeLink to get through that as well, you can turn it on in settings. It's changed only while ringing and put back afterwards.

Not just anyone can wake you

It's an app for waking people up, so consent and control came first.

Both sides agree

You're only connected once the other person types in your invite code. There's no way to add someone behind their back.

Permission per direction

Each of you decides separately, like "I can wake them, but they can't wake me."

Turn it off anytime

Switch the whole app off, block one person, or disconnect completely.

Always visible

A notification always shows when people can wake you. Nothing runs hidden.

No spamming

One wake-up per person every 30 seconds, and at most 20 in 24 hours.

Minimal data

The server stores only your display name and a push notification token. Deleting your account deletes your activity too.

Questions

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. It's Android only, and both the person waking and the person being woken need the app on an Android phone.

What languages is the app in?

English and Korean. It follows your phone's language, and on Android 13 and later you can also pick one just for WakeLink in system settings.

It's not on the Play Store. Is it safe to install?

The APK behind the button above is built from the public source on GitHub and always signed with the same key. The first time, you'll need to let your browser or file manager "install unknown apps".

How do updates work?

When a new version is out, the app tells you when you open it. One tap downloads it and opens the installer. You can also check yourself under Settings → App updates.

What if battery saving stops it from ringing?

Settings → Device readiness checks notifications, the full-screen lock screen alarm and battery optimization. With no warnings there, wake-ups arrive right away even while your phone sleeps.

What if their phone is off when I send a wake-up?

Your screen says it wasn't confirmed. It only says it rang once sound actually starts playing on their phone.

Can I run my own server?

Yes. The server and the app are both open source under the MIT license. Because of how push notifications work, swapping the server alone isn't enough: you also build the app against your own Firebase project. See the self-hosting guide.
